⛩ Wazalist — User Guide

What is this?

Wazalist is a reference database of wotagei Waza. Browse all entries, watch reference videos, and track your learning progress.

Guest vs account

  • Guest — all data saved locally in your browser on this device only.
  • Account — progress syncs across devices.

Browsing & filters

  • Search by Japanese or English name.
  • Filter by skill level, family group, or shape markers via dropdowns.
  • Quick-filter by 👍 Liked button.

Tracking

Use shape markers (● ▲ ■ ♥ ★ ◆) to track your Waza however you like — assign your own meaning to each shape.

Like / Dislike

👍 Like or 👎 Dislike any Waza. Mutually exclusive.

Shape markers

6 shapes (● ▲ ■ ♥ ★ ◆) per Waza. Toggle any combination — meaning is up to you.

Waza detail

Click any Waza to open its detail panel. The Names, Classification, and Reference sections can be collapsed to reduce clutter.

Dashboard

  • Home — overview of your marked and liked Waza.
  • My List — expanded cards for all your tracked Waza, with video buttons directly on each card.
  • Stats — detailed breakdown of your progress.
