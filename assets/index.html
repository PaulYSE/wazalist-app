⛩ Wazalist — User Guide

What is this?

Wazalist is a reference database of wotagei Waza (skills/techniques). Browse all entries, watch reference videos, and track your learning progress. Your data syncs across devices via your account.

Browsing & searching

  • Type in the search bar to filter by Japanese or English name.
  • Use All levels to filter by skill tag.
  • Use All families to filter by parent Waza / family group.
  • Click ★ Bookmarked or ⏳ In progress to filter by those states.
  • Click a Waza in the list to open its full detail view.
  • In the detail view, click a parent chip to filter to that family.

Progress tracking

  • New — default state for all Waza.
  • In progress — you are actively learning this Waza.
  • Completed — you have mastered this Waza.
  • ★ Bookmark — "want to learn" marker, independent of learning stage.

Dashboard

See your progress stats and lists of bookmarked and in-progress Waza. The progress bar tracks completion of your bookmarked Waza only.

Video references

Each Waza may have up to 6 video links. Supported platforms: YouTube, Bilibili, Twitter/X, NicoNico, Facebook.

Account

Progress is saved to your account and syncs across devices. Sign in with your email and PIN from any browser.
