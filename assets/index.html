⛩ Wazalist — User Guide

What is this?

Wazalist is a reference database of wotagei Waza. Browse all entries, watch reference videos, and track your learning progress.

Guest vs account

  • Guest — all data saved locally in your browser on this device only.
  • Account — progress syncs across devices.

Browsing & filters

  • Search by Japanese or English name.
  • Filter by skill level, family group, or markings via dropdowns.
  • Use Any ★ to show only Waza you have marked or liked (your personal list).
  • Sort by Waza Name or Likes in ascending or descending order.

Tracking

Use markings (● ▲ ■ ♥ ★ ◆) to track your Waza however you like — assign your own meaning to each shape.

Like / Dislike

👍 Like or 👎 Dislike any Waza. Mutually exclusive.

Markings

6 shapes (● ▲ ■ ♥ ★ ◆) per Waza. Toggle any combination — meaning is up to you.

Waza detail

Click any Waza to open its detail panel. The Names, Classification, and Reference sections can be collapsed to reduce clutter.

Dashboard

  • Stats — overview counts, shape legend editor, recently updated Waza, and coverage by family.
  • Compare — import and compare another user's list side-by-side with your marks.
  • Contributions — history of edits and new Waza you've submitted (logged-in users).
