Welcome to Wazalist
Your personal database for Wotagei techniques — browse every Waza, track your learning, and build your own list.
This quick tour covers all the key features. It takes about 90 seconds.
🔍
Explore all Waza
Use the Explore tab to search the full database. Every Waza has its Japanese name, English translation, skill level, parent techniques, and reference videos.

Can't write in Japanese?
